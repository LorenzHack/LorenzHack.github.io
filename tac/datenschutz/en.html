In short: without signing in, everything stays on your device.

Games against the AI, training, statistics and settings are stored on your device. There is no tracking, no advertising and no analytics.

DATA STORED LOCALLY

iOS Profile name and picture, your marble and board designs, settings, statistics and games in progress. You can change all of this in the app at any time or remove it completely by deleting the app.

Android Your name, settings, statistics, points and training progress. You can reset all of it at any time or remove it completely by deleting the app.

ONLINE AND ACCOUNT

Online games run on the Tac-With-Bug server. Data is only sent there once you create an account or sign in: when signing up your username, email address and password, when playing your moves, chat messages, friendships and reports. The Tac-With-Bug privacy policy applies to this.

When you are signed in, the app also transfers your profile picture and your “About me” text to your account. Other players see them there in your profile.

https://game.tac-with-bug.org/#/datenschutz

PROGRESS IN YOUR ACCOUNT

When you are signed in, the app also stores your statistics, achievements and training progress in your account, so they come along on other devices and in the browser. You can delete your account at any time in the settings.

SIGN-IN AND NOTIFICATIONS

Your sign-in details are kept encrypted in your device's secure storage so that you stay signed in. Notifications about moves, messages and tournaments are created by the app itself on your device; no push service is used.

FEEDBACK BY EMAIL

iOS When you send feedback, your mail app opens with a prepared draft. Only what you send yourself is transmitted, supplemented with the app and iOS version for troubleshooting.

Android When you send feedback, your mail app opens with a prepared draft. Only what you send yourself is transmitted.
